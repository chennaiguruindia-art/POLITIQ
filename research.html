Research & Public Affairs Practice

Empirical public opinion research, scientific survey design, governance scheme efficacy evaluations, and objective policy impact analyses.

Practice Overview

The Power of Unbiased Empirical Evidence

In public leadership and civic advocacy, assumptions are costly. We replace guesswork with rigorous statistical science.

POLITIQ’s Research & Public Affairs division operates at the intersection of political science, behavioral economics, and advanced survey methodologies. We conduct large-scale, face-to-face and computer-assisted telephone surveys (CATI) designed with multi-stage stratified random sampling to eliminate demographic bias.

Whether auditing the last-mile delivery of public welfare programs or testing voter receptivity to tax or environmental policy reform, our research fellows deliver nuanced findings that inform strategic decisions and public whitepapers.

Methodological Hallmarks

Stratified Random Sampling

Carefully calibrated demographic quotas reflecting census metrics across gender, age, rural/urban density, and income brackets.

Double-Blind Focus Groups

Facilitated focus group discussions conducted without revealing client or party sponsors to elicit unvarnished voter feedback.

Peer-Reviewed Statistical Models

Employing multivariable logistic regression and sentiment analysis to identify underlying drivers of civic satisfaction.
