Institutional Privacy & Legal Disclosures

Our binding commitments regarding client confidentiality, data governance, statutory compliance, and terms of engagement.

Data Protection

1. Privacy Statement & Data Governance

POLITIQ Strategic Advisory & Technology Group ("POLITIQ", "we", "our") maintains the highest standards of informational security, non-disclosure, and ethical integrity. This policy outlines our protocols for handling preliminary inquiries, research data, and institutional correspondence.

1.1 Information Collected Through this Website

This website is a static corporate information portal. We do not deploy third-party advertising trackers, behavioural cookie networks, or cross-site data aggregators. Any contact details submitted voluntarily via our inquiry form or direct email channels are utilized exclusively for scheduling consultations and providing requested briefing materials.

1.2 Survey Research & Voter Privacy

All empirical research and public opinion surveys conducted by our Research Practice comply strictly with national privacy statutes (including GDPR, CCPA, and regional equivalents). Survey respondent participation is strictly voluntary, and all survey findings are anonymized and aggregated to prevent individual re-identification.

Engagement Standards

2. Terms of Advisory Engagement

All commercial and advisory services provided by POLITIQ are subject to formalized, bilateral Master Services Agreements (MSA), Statements of Work (SOW), and comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).

  • Exclusivity & Conflict Checks: Prior to accepting any client mandate, our ethics committee conducts thorough conflict-of-interest checks to ensure non-overlapping geographic exclusivity within competitive electoral jurisdictions.
  • Client Data Ownership: All proprietary databases, voter models, and software configurations engineered during client engagements remain the exclusive intellectual property of the commissioning client.
  • Zero Third-Party Data Monetization: We never sell, lease, or syndicate client datasets to external political parties, commercial advertisers, or research brokers.
Legal Compliance

3. Statutory Compliance & Non-Guarantee Statement

3.1 Adherence to Election Commissions

POLITIQ conducts its consultancy operations in strict conformity with national and state election commissions, campaign finance disclosure mandates, and advertising pre-certification laws. We do not participate in or endorse unlawful campaign activities, vote purchasing, unauthorized voter roll manipulation, or unlawful surveillance.

3.2 Professional Outcomes Disclaimer

POLITIQ provides high-level strategic advisory, research diagnostics, digital communications, and technology solutions based on scientific methodologies. However, in accordance with global consulting standards, POLITIQ does not and cannot guarantee specific election results, ballot percentages, or political outcomes. Final outcomes in a democracy rest solely with sovereign voters.

3.3 Illustrative Content Disclaimer

All case summaries, project archives, and operational photographs displayed on this website are intended solely for illustrative demonstration of capabilities. Identifying client data has been anonymized to protect institutional confidentiality.
