Release of the 2026 Civic Sentiment & Municipal Delivery Index

Our quantitative research division has published the 2026 Civic Sentiment Index, evaluating citizen satisfaction across 60 municipal wards using stratified random sampling of 48,000 households.

The report highlights emerging voter concerns regarding clean transit access, digital public grievance resolution times, and the rising salience of localized environmental governance.
